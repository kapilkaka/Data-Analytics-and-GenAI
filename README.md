# Retail Analytics: Predicting Customer Purchase Behavior

## 📌 Project Overview
This capstone project focuses on analyzing retail transaction data to **predict customer purchase behavior** and **campaign response rates**. By leveraging demographic information, purchasing patterns, and marketing campaign interactions, the project provides actionable insights to optimize promotional strategies and maximize ROI.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- **Customer Segmentation**: Categorize customers based on demographics, purchasing power, and loyalty.
- **Campaign Prediction**: Build a foundation for predicting customer responses to marketing campaigns.
- **ROI Optimization**: Identify which channels (Web, Catalog, Store) yield the highest returns for specific demographics.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Excel** → Data exploration, statistical summaries, charts, and boxplots.
- **SQL (MySQL)** → Data preprocessing, schema creation, queries for customer insights.
- **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)** → Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), univariate & bivariate analysis, outlier detection.
- **Tableau** → Interactive dashboards for customer segmentation, income distribution, and purchase frequency.

---

## 📊 Key Tasks
### Excel
- Statistical summary of numerical features.
- Line chart for customer enrolments by year.
- Cross-tabulation of campaign responses vs. education.
- Boxplot analysis of income distribution.
- Histogram of customer age.
- Visualization of campaign response vs. marital status.

### SQL
- Schema creation (`retail_data`) and data loading.
- Top 10 purchased products (Wines, Meat, Fish, Gold, etc.).
- Campaign response distribution and acceptance rates.
- Customer segmentation by education and marital status.
- Average income analysis of campaign participants.
- Promotions accepted across campaigns.
- Age group creation and monthly visit analysis.

### Python
- Data overview, missing value checks, and descriptive statistics.
- Univariate and bivariate analysis (histograms, scatter plots, chi-square tests).
- Outlier detection using boxplots.
- Correlation analysis between income and spending.

### Tableau
- Income distribution grouped by year of registration.
- Education & marital status breakdown.
- Relationship between income and wine spending.
- Purchase frequency across product categories.
- Executive dashboard combining all visualizations.

---

## 📈 Insights & Outcomes
- Identified **top product categories** (Wines, Meat, Gold) driving revenue.
- Campaign acceptance rate: **~15%** of customers responded positively.
- Income distribution revealed **extreme outliers** skewing averages.
- Customer age segmentation highlighted **core demographics (36–55 years)** as high-value.
- Tableau dashboards provided **clear evidence for marketing ROI optimization**.
